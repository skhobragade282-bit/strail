Strail Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

Strail collects information you provide directly, including business name, contact details, client information, session data, and payment records. This data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to operate the Strail app: managing sessions, tracking fee payments, generating reminders, and organizing trainer payouts. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device. You are responsible for maintaining backups through Apple-supported backup services such as iCloud or device backups. Strail does not operate its own cloud servers for your studio data.

4. WhatsApp Integration

When you send reminders through WhatsApp, the message is passed to the WhatsApp app on your device. Strail does not store or transmit your clients' phone numbers to an external Strail server.

5. Sign in with Apple

Strail uses Sign in with Apple for authentication. We receive only the information Apple provides, such as name and email address. We do not store passwords.

6. Children's Privacy

Strail is designed for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Help & Support section in Strail Settings.
