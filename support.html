Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a client?

Go to the Clients tab and tap the + button in the top right corner. Enter the client's name and details, then tap Save.

How do I mark a client's payment?

Open the client's profile and navigate to the Payments section. Tap the payment entry and change its status to Paid, Pending, or Overdue.

How do I send a WhatsApp reminder to a client?

Open the client's profile and tap the WhatsApp icon. Strail will open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. You can edit it before sending.

How do I manage trainer payments?

Go to the Team section, open the trainer's profile, and navigate to their Payments tab. You can record and track salary payments from there.

How do I create a session?

Go to the Sessions tab and tap +. Choose the session type, assign clients or trainers, set the date and time, then tap Save.

How do I start my free trial?

Go to Settings → Subscription and tap "Start Free Trial." You get 21 days free before any charge. Cancel anytime from your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

How do I restore my subscription after reinstalling?

Go to Settings → Subscription and tap "Restore Purchase." Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used when you originally subscribed.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your iPhone Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Strail → Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

Is my data backed up?

Strail stores all data locally on your device. Your data is included in your iCloud or device backup if you have backups enabled in iPhone Settings.
